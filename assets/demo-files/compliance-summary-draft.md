# Summary for the Compliance Team (FICTIONAL training file)

The Workplace Data Retention Rule 2026 applies to all organizations that store customer records electronically, regardless of size (Section 3.1).

Key requirements:

- Customer contact records must be deleted within **12 months** of the last transaction (Section 4.1).
- Financial transaction records must be kept for 7 years (Section 4.2).
- Every access to customer records must be logged, and logs must be kept for 3 years (Section 5.2).
- Organizations must appoint a Data Retention Officer by January 2027 (Section 8.2).

Penalties start at $50,000 per violation and rise to $200,000 for repeat violations (Section 6).

The rule takes effect on March 1, 2027. Since smaller companies typically have weaker data practices, the compliance team should focus audits on them first.
