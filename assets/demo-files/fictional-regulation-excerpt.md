# Workplace Data Retention Rule 2026 (FICTIONAL — for training only)

> This regulation does not exist. It is a training document for practicing output validation.

## Section 3 — Scope

3.1 This Rule applies to organizations with **250 or more employees** that store customer records in electronic form.

3.2 Organizations with fewer than 250 employees are exempt from Sections 5 and 6, but must comply with Section 4.

## Section 4 — Retention limits

4.1 Customer contact records must be deleted no later than **24 months** after the customer's last transaction.

4.2 Financial transaction records must be retained for **7 years**, then deleted within 90 days.

4.3 Records subject to an active legal hold are exempt from 4.1 and 4.2 until the hold is lifted.

## Section 5 — Access logging

5.1 Every access to customer records by an employee must be logged with the employee ID, timestamp, and purpose.

5.2 Access logs must be retained for **3 years**.

## Section 6 — Penalties

6.1 A first violation carries a fine of up to **$50,000**.

6.2 Repeat violations within 5 years carry a fine of up to **$200,000** per violation.

## Section 7 — Effective date

7.1 This Rule takes effect on **March 1, 2027**.
